# Implementation

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Define the scope of implementation

* Start by choosing a single commodity and identifying which producers the volumes come from.
* Then select the producers in scope. When selecting producers, we recommend first selecting those with whom companies have a strong relationship. If Due Diligence Reports indicate potential labor rights at risk for certain commodities or countries, those should be included as priority.
* If there are many producers in the company’s supply chain (i.e. > 200), it is recommended to divide the effort of rolling out the Salary Matrix over a period of 2 or 3 years. For instance, living wage gaps could be measured for at least one third of volumes each year over a period of 3 years. That means, producers who supply one third of the purchasing volumes should apply the Salary Matrix in year 1. By the second year, living wage gaps should be measured for at least two thirds of volumes. After three years, all volumes should be measured.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Implement the tool

* Introduce the company’s efforts on living wages to the selected producers.
* It is recommended to involve worker representatives in the process of completing the Salary Matrix, to ensure that the data accurately reflects the reality of workers in the facilities.
* Ask selected producers to compare remuneration against living wage estimates in their facilities using the Salary Matrix and provide them with the required training and support.
* Next, it is essential to ensure the quality of the data with the support of a third party. Conduct on-site (preferably) or remote verification data checks with a sample of users, to ensure the data was entered correctly into the digital Salary Matrix.
* Auditing of the Salary Matrix is also highly recommended to ensure validity of the data. To read more about our verification see [Step 3.](https://www.idhsustainabletrade.com/living-wage-platform/verifying-calculations/)
* The living wage gap calculation report can be shared with the company by the producer. IDH Solutions can also provide support to companies to aggregate living wage gaps in the entire supply chain and develop projects to take action to close the gaps. To read more about taking action, see [Step 4.](https://www.idhsustainabletrade.com/living-wage-platform/closethegap/)
* Continue to measure gaps each year to track progress towards closing living wage gaps.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
