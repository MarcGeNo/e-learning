# Disclaimers

By taking into consideration the % of year worked, the Salary Matrix prorates the remuneration of workers who worked part-time, or did not work all year-round, to a full-time equivalent (FTE) comparable remuneration.

This means that the tool calculates the monthly remuneration that the workers would have earned if they had been working full-time during the entire reporting year and under the same retribution scheme (receiving the same wages, bonuses and in-kind benefits).

The tool then compares this prorated comparable remuneration against the living wage estimate calculated for formal hired workers that enjoy the benefits of contributions paid by their employer to public social security services.

This means that the absence of a living wage gap does not mean for all workers that they **actually earned a living wage** due to the below reasons:
