# Closing Living Wage Gaps

The information in your report will provide you with suitable metrics to guide your closing gap programa.

For example, if you have selected the Gap per trade unit option for your report, the Gap per trade unit will be included under the Product information section of the report, allowing you to calculate the Contribution amount necessary to close the living wage gap based for the purchaed amount.

To find out  how you can take action with your supply chain partners to close living wage gaps see the [Step 4](https://www.idhsustainabletrade.com/living-wage-platform/closethegap/) of the living wage road map.
