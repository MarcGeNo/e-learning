# Additional Support and Services

Please contact the helpdesk at [livingwagematrix@idhtrade.org](mailto:livingwagematrix@idhtrade.org) for:

* **Questions**: if you do not find an answer to your questions about the use of the Salary Matrix or have comments about this e-learning site.
* **Data checks:** If you are interested in a quote to get the data entered in your calculations checked for potential mistakes.
* **Aggregated reports**: If you are interested in a quote for receiving aggregate reports for the facilities in your supply chain. As an additional service, the data from the IDH Salary Matrix can be aggregated (and anonymized where relevant) by IDH Solutions to draw insights and spot patterns that can empower suppliers and buyers in the living wage transformation.&#x20;
* **Living wage gap consultancy**: If you are interested in a quote for getting in support for your living wage program.
