# Tool navigation

### The left navigation panel: top section

The top section of the left navigation panel allows you to choose between three different visualization options:

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (11)" alt=""></div>

<details>

<summary>Dashboard</summary>

The **Dashboard** view provides a quick view of the number of Facilities, Calculations and Reports created.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (13)>)You can create a Living Wage Gap Calculation by clicking on the top right button.

You can also view the latest edited Facilities and a list of the latest updated Living Wage Gap Calculations.

You can access one Facility by clicking on the corresponding facility card to edit the Facility or create a New Calculation for the Facility.

You can open a Calculation by clicking on the corresponding Calculation line. The three dots at the end of the line provide additional options.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (14)>)

</details>

<details>

<summary>Facilities</summary>

The **Facilities** view show all your Facilities as cards including details about Sector, Products, Facility ID and the number of Calculations linked to each facility.&#x20;

You can create a new Facility by clicking the Create Facility button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Captura de pantalla 2026-01-22 a les 17.57.32.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can access the Facility by clicking on the corresponding facility card. This will allow you  to edit or delete the Facility, or to create a New Calculation linked to the Facility.

</details>

<details>

<summary> Calculations</summary>

The **Calculations** view provides a quick view of the number of Living wage gap calculations created and their current status and a list of your Calculations. You can also **Export the list** of Calculations to a csv file and **Create a living wage gap calculation**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (17)>)

Use the filters to identify the calculation you want to view. You can filter by installation name,

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (18)>)

You can open a calculation by clicking on the corresponding calculation line. Click on the three dots at the end of the line to Edit, Copy or Archive a calculation.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (14)>)

</details>

### The left navigation panel: bottom section

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Captura de pantalla 2026-02-16 a les 18.28.29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The lower section allows you to access tool support pages and your profile:

<details>

<summary>Announcements</summary>

The Salary Matrix evolves and improves in an on-going basis based on the feedback received from users and stakeholders. You can see a summary of the latest changes by clicking the **Announcements** icon at the bottom of the left navigation panel.

</details>

<details>

<summary>E-learning</summary>

The **E-learning** button takes you to this e-learning site.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Contact support</summary>

If you have read the content of this e-learning site and still have questions, you can address your query to the tool helpdesk by clicking on the **Contact support** button at the bottom of the left navigation panel.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Profile</summary>

By clicking in the arrows on the right of the Profile box, you can access your profile settings or logout.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Captura de pantalla 2026-01-20 a les 15.58.20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On your profile setting, you can change your name and organisation, add a telephone number or change the platform language to any of the languages available.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (20)>)

You can also update your password and change the email linked to your account.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (21)>)

</details>
