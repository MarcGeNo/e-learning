| <p>The normal weekly daytime work schedule will be 45 hours of effective work, equivalent to 48 hours for the sole purpose of calculating wages.</p><p>This provision does not apply to agricultural and livestock workers, nor to those employed by companies with fewer than 10 workers, whose normal weekly daytime work schedule will be 48 hours of effective work, unless a more favorable custom exists for the worker.</p><p>However, this exception should not be extended to agricultural companies with 500 or more workers.</p> | <p>The tool calculates full-time hours based on a maximum of 48 regular weekly hours.</p><p>To prevent the Salary Matrix from artificially inflating employee compensation, facilities with a standard 45-hour work week must adjust the "regular hours worked" as follows:</p><ul><li>Regular hours worked = Hours worked * 48 / 45</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
