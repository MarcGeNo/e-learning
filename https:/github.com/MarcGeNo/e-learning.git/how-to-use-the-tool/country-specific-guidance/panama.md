# 🇵🇦 Panamá

| **Bonuses and benefits**                                                                                                                                                                                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <p><br>Annual leave</p><p>30 days of paid holiday.</p><p>Compensation with remuneration is not permitted.</p>                                                                                                                | <p><strong>Guidance</strong>: the amounts received corresponding to enjoyed annual leave and public holidays should be included in the “Wages earned” section. </p><p></p><p><strong>WHY</strong>: expected and guaranteed remuneration.</p>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| <p>Aguinaldo (13th month) </p><p>Not required by law.</p>                                                                                                                                                                    | <p><strong>Recommendation</strong>: It CAN ONLY be included if the payment is regulated through the collective bargaining agreement and if it has been paid consistently during the last 5 years. </p><p>In this case, enter in Bonuses as 13th, 14th, 15th month payment. Enter the paid amount excluding any amount corresponding to overtime hours. The tool will calculate the monthly full-time equivalent</p><p></p><p><strong>WHY</strong>: only expected and guaranteed amounts can count towards a living wage.</p> |
| <p>Disability leave fund </p><p>Twelve hours for every twenty-six days served or one hundred and forty-four hours a year.</p>                                                                                                | <p><strong>Recommendation</strong>: it should NOT be included </p><p></p><p><strong>WHY</strong>: it is a safety net in case of illness or non-occupational accident.</p>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| <p>Cesantía</p><p>Only for permanent workers. </p><p>It is used to pay the seniority premium and severance pay. The part corresponding to the seniority premium can be pledged as collateral for the purchase of a home.</p> | <p><strong>Recommendation</strong>: it should NOT be included. Only voluntary contributions from the company above the established legal minimum could be included. </p><p></p><p><strong>WHY</strong>: it is not a yearly payment and the cost to purchase a house is not included in the calculations of the living wage estimate.</p>                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| <p>Transport </p><p>Required by law for farms with more than 10 workers if they are more than 5 km from the habitual residence.</p>                                                                                          | <p><strong>Recommendation</strong>: it CAN be included as time-dependent bonus when paid on cash, or as in-kind benefit when transport is provided.</p><p></p><p><strong>WHY</strong>: covers a cost included in the estimation of the living wage estimate.</p>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
