# Entering wages

{% hint style="info" %}
Enter values per worker per year.

The Salary Matrix requires numbers to be entered without thousand’s separators and decimals separated by a dot. Thus, the correct format would be: 5000.55.

Remember to use the same currency unit than the unit of the selected living wage estimate.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:red;">New: Hours and remuneration during</mark> [<mark style="color:red;">paid personal leave</mark>](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] <mark style="color:red;">must now be entered as time worked</mark>
{% endhint %}

<details>

<summary>Ordinary hours worked – Standard work year</summary>

Enter the total number of ordinary hours worked by the individual workers, or the average of the job category. The tool will calculate the percentage of year worked and adjust the calculations to the standard work year.

The total number of ordinary hours worked includes:

* Regular worked hours
* Hours when the worker was on paid personal leave (sick, parental, bereavement, seniority days off)
* Rest breaks during the work shift
* Mandatory training hours
* Travel time during normal work hours required for a job assignment, such as travel between job sites.
* On-call time when workers cannot use their time as they wish.

This does not include:

* Hours of paid annual leave, public holidays or weekly rest days
* Hours of voluntary unpaid license
* Lunch break hours outside the work shift

</details>

<details>

<summary>Ordinary hours worked – Restricted workweek/year</summary>

For some workers, the legally allowed regular working hours may be lower than the standard working hours. This can apply to:

* Workers with reduced working hours due to health and safety risks (e.g. fumigators, night shift workers, or piece-rate workers in strenuous conditions); or​
* Workers covered by laws or collective bargaining agreements (CBAs) that set shorter regular working hours than the standard work week or year.​

To prevent the tool from adjusting these worker's comparable remuneration up to the standard work-year, the below adjustments are necessary:

a) If the reduction is applicable to all workers in the facility:

Add the number of annual reduction hours (Standard full-time work year - Actual reduced full-time work year) to the "Minimum number of hours of paid annual leave".

For example, if the standard workweek is 48 hours, but the reduced workweek for the facility is 40 hours, add (48-40) \* 52,143 hours to the _Minimum number of hours of paid annual leave_ (including paid annual leave and holidays).

Then, add payroll data (hours, wages, bonuses, and in-kind benefits) as you would normally.

**b) If the reduction is applicable only to certain job categories/individual workers**,

The "Ordinary hours worked" of the workers in that job category shall be adjusted as follows:

* Ordinary hours worked \* Standard workweek / Actual restricted workweek

For example, if the standard workweek is 48 hours, but the reduced workweek for the job category/worker is 40 hours, and the ordinary hours worked are 2000, use 2000 \* 48 / 40 = 2400, for ordinary hours worked.

Then, add the other payroll data (wages, bonuses, and in-kind benefits) as you would normally.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Wage earned</summary>

Enter here the **yearly** cash wages earned by the individual workers, or the average of the Job category.

This includes:

* Base wage earned during the standard workweek or below 48 hours, whichever is lower.
* Pay corresponding to annual leave and public holidays
* Compensation received during remunerated personal leaves (e.g. sick or parental leaves), either paid by the employer or received from public social security systems

This does not include:

* 13th, 14<sup>th</sup>, 15<sup>th</sup> month payments
* Bonuses
* Overtime pay

</details>

<details>

<summary>Overtime hours worked</summary>

Enter here the **yearly** number of overtime hours worked by the individual workers, or the average of the job category.

This includes:

* Hours worked beyond the standard workweek or 48 hours, whichever is lower
* Hours worked during annual leave or public holidays compensated with additional remuneration

{% hint style="info" %}
If overtime hours have not been paid, they must be included as ordinary hours.
{% endhint %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Overtime pay earned</summary>

Enter here the cash wages earned for overtime work by the individual workers, or the average of the Job category.

This includes:

* Overtime pay for hours worked beyond the standard workweek or 48 hours, whichever is lower
* Cash compensation for hours worked during annual leave or public holidays

</details>



[^1]: Paid time-off to cater for specific personal circumstances that workers are entitled to per year according to local labour laws or company policy (e.g. sick/disability leave, family care, bereavement leave, union representation days...).
