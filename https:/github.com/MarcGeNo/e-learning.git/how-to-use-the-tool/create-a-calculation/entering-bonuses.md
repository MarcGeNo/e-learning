# Entering bonuses

{% hint style="info" %}
Enter values per worker per year.

Make sure to include only bonuses earned when working during regular wokring hours. Bonuses earned on overtime and productivty bonuses that cannot be earned by the majority of workers working at a normal work pace should not be entered.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
<mark style="color:$danger;">New: The tool now prorates time-dependent bonuses based on the number of full-time days worked.</mark>
{% endhint %}

For calculation and verification purposes, the tool requires to classify bonuses in three different types.

#### 13th, 14th and 15th month bonuses

In some countries, it is a legal requirement that employers pay workers one, two or even three months of additional salary, beyond the 12 calendar months. These additional months are considered fixed bonuses and are included together in the field "13th, 14th and/or 15th-month payment".

The amount of these bonuses is usually equivalent to an average monthly salary or the minimum salary.

{% hint style="info" %}
Enter the actual yearly amount paid to the worker (or the average per worker for the job category), excluding any amounts resulting from overtime, in spite that national legislation might sometimes require to include overtime payments in the bonus calculation.
{% endhint %}

The tool automatically prorates the amount to full-time-equivalent for part-time workers or workers that did not work all year-round.

#### Time-dependent bonuses

Time-dependent bonuses are additional cash payments to one’s normal rate that vary with the number of hours/days worked during the reporting year. These bonuses can be variable incentives based on some measure of performance (for example, attendance bonuses, productivity bonuses and quality bonuses) or fixed allowances depending on the number of days worked (transport allowance).

Enter the total yearly amount actually paid to the worker, or the average per worker for the job category.

{% hint style="info" %}
Include only bonuses earned working at a normal pace during a 48-hour workweek or during the standard workweek, whichever is less.
{% endhint %}

The tool automatically prorates the amount to full-time-equivalent for part-time workers or workers that did not work all year-round.

#### Non-time dependent bonuses

Non-time dependent bonuses are cash payments, in addition to one’s normal rate, the amount of which is fixed and does not depend on the number of days worked. These bonuses are usually paid annually or semi-annually, rather than continuously and are tied to time of the year, rather than performance. For example, a seasonal bonus.

Non-time dependent bonuses do not need to be prorated to full year. Enter the total yearly amount actually paid to the worker, or the average per worker for the job category.
