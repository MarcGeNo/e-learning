# Reports

The content of your report will depend on the Add ons selected.

The report includes a graph where you can easily see the job categories above or below the living wage estimate and the contribution of wages, bonuses and in-kind benefits on the comparable monthly remuneration.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (33)>)

If you identify the need to make any corrections on the data entered, you can always reopen your Calculation by clicking on the three dots on the top of the page and selecting _Reopen to edit_. Once the data have been corrected, you can resubmit the data to access the corrected final report.

By clicking on the three dots, you also have the option to _Archive_ a Calculation that is no longer required.

If you want to share your report, click on the button _Share Report_ next to the three dots. You will be able to download your report in PDF and Excel formats.
