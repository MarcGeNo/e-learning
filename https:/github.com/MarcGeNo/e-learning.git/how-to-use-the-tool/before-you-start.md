# Before you start

{% hint style="info" %}
### Information needed to have at hand before starting

The Salary Matrix must be completed with the most recent full calendar year of production and salary data.

To get accurate calculations, you need to have:

* **for each worker who has worked in the facility during the period of data entry:**
  * The number of worked hours (regular and overtime)
  * The wages and bonuses received
  * The in-kind benefits received
* **for the facility**
  * The minimum number of hours of paid annual leave for a worker
  * The number of hours of unpaid mandatory leave for a worker
  * The annual cost to the employer of the different in-kind benefits per worker
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Create an account

* To create an account, go to the [Salary Matrix](https://www.salarymatrix.idhtrade.org/).

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (9)>)

* The tool is currently available in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Vietnamese. You can change language by clicking on the top right button
* If this is your first time accessing the Salary Matrix, please click on the ‘Sign up’ button on the top right.
* Enter an Email address and a safe password. We recommend the use of a company email address that is not linked to a specific person. This ensures you maintain access to your calculations regardless of any staff changes.
* Click Next.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Unknown image (10)>)

* You will then be asked to confirm the Confidentiality and Data Compliance Statement. You need to scroll down to the end of the statement and click on the “Agree and Sign up” button.
* Check your email to verify your email address.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Log in

* Go to the [Salary Matrix](https://www.salarymatrix.idhtrade.org/).
* Log in with your e-mail and password.
* If you forgot your password or your password is not working, click on "Forgot Password". You should receive an email to create a new password.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
